Holiday Inn Cardiff City Centre

Our base for the weekend was the Holiday Inn Cardiff City Centre, perfectly positioned in one of the most enviable locations in the city. Overlooking the historic grounds of Cardiff Castle, with breathtaking views across the Castle, Cardiff Arms Park, and the River Taff, the hotel offers a truly memorable setting for your stay.

Just moments from the Principality Stadium and a short stroll from Cardiff’s renowned shopping arcades, cafés, and restaurants, the hotel places you right at the heart of the city’s vibrant atmosphere.

Whether you choose to explore the historic streets, relax by the riverside, or take in the scenery from the comfort of the hotel, everything is conveniently within easy reach.

Comfort & Convenience

The hotel offers a welcoming and comfortable environment, perfectly suited for a relaxing and enjoyable stay throughout the Reunion weekend. Guests can expect:

  • Modern, well-appointed bedrooms with Wi-Fi and air conditioning
  • An on-site restaurant and bar, many areas offering views across the Castle grounds and surrounding landmarks
  • Secure on-site parking (subject to availability)
  • Excellent transport links, with Cardiff Central Station just a short walk away
  • Easy access to the waterfront attractions of Cardiff Bay

A Perfect Base for the Weekend

More than simply a place to stay, the Holiday Inn Cardiff City Centre serves as the social heart of the Reunion — a place where conversations continue long after the formal programme has ended.

Whether gathering for a drink, meeting friends before events, or unwinding after a full day of activity, the hotel provides a relaxed and welcoming environment for both brethren and guests. Its central location makes it effortless to move between venues, while also offering the opportunity to step away and enjoy the surrounding views or quiet moments by the River Taff.

With its unique combination of breathtaking outlooks, modern comfort, and exceptional convenience, the hotel creates the perfect setting for a weekend defined by fellowship, connection, and shared experience.
