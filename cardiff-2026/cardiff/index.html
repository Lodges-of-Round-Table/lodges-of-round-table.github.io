A Capital of Culture, History & Warmth

Cardiff, the capital of Wales, is a city that blends rich history with a vibrant modern spirit. From its medieval castle at the heart of the city to the cutting-edge architecture of Cardiff Bay, the city offers something for everyone.

Landmarks & Attractions

  • Cardiff Castle — Over 2,000 years of history, from Roman walls to a Victorian Gothic revival mansion.
  • Principality Stadium — The home of Welsh rugby and one of the world’s most impressive sporting arenas.
  • Cardiff Bay — A stunning waterfront development with the Wales Millennium Centre, the Senedd (Welsh Parliament), and a host of restaurants and attractions.
  • Victorian Arcades & Shopping — Quaint covered arcades and bustling shopping streets filled with independent stores, cafés, and boutiques.
  • Museums & Galleries — Including the free National Museum Cardiff, with art, natural history, and world-class exhibitions.

A Walkable, Welcoming City

One of Cardiff’s greatest charms is its size. Compact and easy to explore on foot, the city centre, Bay, and cultural landmarks are all within easy reach. The people of Cardiff are renowned for their friendliness and hospitality, ensuring visitors feel at home straight away.

Food & Nightlife

Whether you’re looking for fine dining, lively bars, or a quiet riverside pub, Cardiff offers a wealth of choices. The city is proud of its local produce and vibrant dining scene.

Beyond the City

For those extending their stay, Cardiff is the gateway to South Wales. Within minutes you can explore the Brecon Beacons National Park, the Glamorgan Heritage Coast, and the rolling countryside of the Vale of Glamorgan.
