Welcome to Cardiff 2026

The Lodge of Round Table No. 9549 was delighted to host the Lodges of Round Table Biennial Reunion from Friday 15th to Sunday 17th May 2026 in the heart of Cardiff.

The Reunion brought together members, partners, and friends from Lodges of Round Table across the country and beyond: a special occasion to rekindle friendships, celebrate our shared values, and enjoy a weekend of fellowship in one of the UK’s most vibrant cities.

Our base for the weekend was the Holiday Inn Cardiff City Centre, opposite Cardiff Castle and just a short walk from the Principality Stadium and Cardiff Bay.

The programme

  • Friday evening: A Lodge Installation Meeting and dinner at Cardiff Masonic Hall for brethren, and a private ladies’ dinner at the hotel with after-dinner entertainment from the man with the ‘Rolls-Royce Voice’, West End & Opera Sensation Anthony Stuart Lloyd.
  • Saturday: A private tour of Cardiff Castle with lunch in the hospitality suite, free time to explore the city or take a boat trip around the Bay, followed by a black-tie gala dinner at Cornerstone Cardiff with headline entertainment from Wales’ Premiere Showman and Entertainer, The Man, The Hat, The Voice, Deano Martini, and a different programme from Anthony Stuart Lloyd.
  • Sunday: A fond farewell after breakfast and time together with friends old and new.
