Friendship, tradition and the spirit of Round Table

Fellowship that began at the Round Table

The Lodges of Round Table are a network of Masonic lodges, mostly in England and Wales with some overseas, founded by members of the Round Table movement. Each lodge is independent, but we share a common origin and come together every two years for a Biennial Reunion hosted by one of the lodges.

These pages will grow into a directory of the lodges around the country. If your lodge belongs on the list, please get in touch.
